## 1. Create the channel

1. Go to **youtube.com** and sign in with the Google account the channel should belong to. A personal account is better than a work account. Uploading from a company laptop may break company policy, so use your own device if unsure.
2. Click your profile picture, then **Create a channel**.
3. Enter the name **Mrs. Money Mind** and the handle **@mrsmoneymind**, upload `profile-800x800.png`, then click **Create channel**.
4. Open **YouTube Studio** (studio.youtube.com) and go to **Customisation**:
   - **Branding:** upload `banner-2560x1440.png`. Keep the crop as it is and click Done.
   - **Basic info:** paste the description and links from section 2.
   - Click **Publish**.
5. Go to **Settings**, then **Channel**:
   - **Basic info:** country = your country. Keywords = from section 2.
   - **Advanced settings:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids". The topic is money and adults.
6. Go to **Settings**, then **Upload defaults**: paste the default description footer from section 2. Category = Education. Language = English.
7. **Verify the phone number** (Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility). This unlocks custom thumbnails and videos longer than 15 minutes.
