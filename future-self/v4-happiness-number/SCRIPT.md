# The Happiness Number (v1 script)

About 590 spoken words. Expect roughly 3:20 of voice, based on the pace of videos 2 and 3. Voice only (Kokoro), and each line has a **mood** (curious, shocked, firm, excited, sad, whisper, warm or neutral).
The narrator talks to the viewer. The screen never reads the line back. Pop words are in **bold**.
The look and characters are the same as videos 1–3: the kid in the mustard hoodie, navy and orange scenes, handwritten pop words. The researchers are shown as **silhouettes** (no likenesses of real people), with name labels only.
This video is mostly charts, so one recurring prop carries it: **the happiness line**, a hand-drawn orange line on a chalk-style graph. Income runs along the bottom and happiness up the side.

## Fact sources
- Kahneman, D. & Deaton, A. (2010). High income improves evaluation of life but not emotional well-being. *PNAS* 107(38): 16489–16493.
  - More than 450,000 responses to the Gallup-Healthways Well-Being Index (US, 2008–2009). Emotional well-being rose with log income but stopped rising at about $75,000 a year. Life evaluation kept rising.
- Killingsworth, M. A. (2021). Experienced well-being rises with log income. *PNAS* 118(4): e2016976118.
  - 1,725,994 experience-sampling reports from 33,391 employed US adults. The slope above $80,000 was as steep as below it.
  - The Penn press release says sense of control explained 74% of the income–well-being link. I couldn't confirm that number in the paper, so the script says "a big part" and doesn't quote it.
- Killingsworth, M. A., Kahneman, D. & Mellers, B. (2023). Income and emotional well-being: A conflict resolved. *PNAS* 120(10): e2208661120.
  - An adversarial collaboration with Mellers as arbiter. For the least happy ~20%, happiness rose until about $100,000 and then plateaued. For the rest, it kept rising, and for the happiest group the rise sped up above $100,000.
  - It explains the 2010 flattening as an unhappiness measure that mostly reflected that unhappy minority.
- Kahneman won the 2002 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences.

**Before building:** check the 2023 paper's own figures for the middle and top groups (press coverage differs on the details). The script only claims "for most people, it kept rising", which every source agrees on.
