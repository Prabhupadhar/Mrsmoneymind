## 3. Fact check (for pinned comments or replies)
- One Canadian province, 2004-2014, prizes from C$1,000 to C$150,000. A postal code there has a median of 13 households (Agarwal, Mikhed & Scholnick).
- A win equal to the median yearly income (C$29,229) raised neighbors' bankruptcies by 6.59% over three years. The video rounds this to "nearly seven percent" and shows the check as $29,000.
- Bankrupt neighbors had more visible assets (house, car), not more cash or pensions. Borrowing rose across the neighborhood.
- Dutch Postcode Lottery: neighbors of winners spent more on cars (Kuhn et al.).
- Higher neighbor earnings were linked to lower self-reported happiness at the same own income (Luttmer).
- Bankruptcy is rare, so 6.59% more of a small number is still small. The video says this in the fact-check section.
