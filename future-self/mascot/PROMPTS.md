# Mrs. Money Mind character: image-generator prompts

These are for an original character in the style of the references (a flat-vector stock woman and a semi-real anime business woman).
They are not copies of those images. Use one tool and keep using it, so the character stays consistent.

## 1. Master image (generate first, pick the best one)

**Style A: flat vector (like the orange-sweater reference)**
> Full-body 2D vector illustration of a confident, friendly woman in her late 20s, standing relaxed with hands in pockets,
> long voluminous wavy auburn hair, warm brown eyes, soft natural makeup, gentle smile, orange crew-neck sweater with
> pushed-up sleeves, high-waist grey jeans with a dark brown belt, brown ankle boots. Clean flat-vector style with soft
> gradient shading, crisp edges, realistic proportions, modern stock-illustration look, plain off-white background,
> soft oval floor shadow, centered, front view, high detail, no text.

**Style B: semi-real anime (like the business-woman reference)**
> Full-body semi-realistic anime illustration of a warm, smart woman in her early 30s, shoulder-length wavy dark plum
> hair in a loose low ponytail, green eyes, small gold drop earrings, soft smile, cropped taupe blazer with rolled
> sleeves over a navy top, cream high-waist wide-leg trousers with a fabric tie belt, black wristwatch, holding a
> clipboard against her side. Soft cel shading, delicate line art, pastel pink gradient background, front view, no text.

**For an Indian look, swap the hair, face and outfit:**
> ...South Asian woman, long dark hair in a loose braid over one shoulder, small bindi, teal cotton kurta with gold
> trim over slim jeans, kolhapuri sandals...

## 2. Consistency: same woman, new poses and expressions
Upload the master image as the character reference (Midjourney `--cref <url> --cw 100`, or attach it in ChatGPT, Gemini or Ideogram), then:
> Same woman, same outfit and hairstyle, character turnaround sheet: front, three-quarter, side, back. Plain background.

> Same woman, expression sheet, 6 head-and-shoulders portraits: worried looking at her phone, guilty grin holding a
> shopping bag, shocked, calm with coffee, proud holding a notebook, thinking. Plain background, consistent face.

## 3. Motion
- Image-to-video (Kling, Hailuo, Runway, Veo): upload a pose and describe one action per clip, 5 s, for example
  "she looks at her phone, winces, then laughs", or "she throws a fast punch, hair whipping, camera shakes".
- Or send the PNGs here: they get layered into HyperFrames Shorts, with captions, charts and voice around them.
